# Research notes — 18 November 2026

Researched on 18 November 2026 (morning UTC) by web search. Only stories
verified as happening or first reported on 18 November 2026, in some time
zone, were used. Several overnight stories were called or struck late on
17 November in the Americas and reported on 18 November UTC / in Asia;
their reporting dates are noted below. Nothing was invented: every number
and name on the poster comes from the sources listed.

## Main stories

1. **US strikes Qeshm Island; drones at Bahrain and Kuwait.** A US
   official told AFP that US fighter jets used precision munitions
   against an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island. Kuwait's army
   said early on Wednesday 18 November (about 01:39 Gulf time) that its
   air defences were countering hostile drones; Bahrain reported
   intercepting further Iranian aerial attacks early on 18 November.
   Sources: Business Today (18 Nov), The Geneva Times (18 Nov 01:45 UTC),
   News Central TV (18 Nov 03:33), One Turn daily edition for 18 Nov
   (citing Japan Times, CBS News, Al Jazeera).
2. **Kerch Bridge road span falls.** Ukraine struck the bridge linking
   Russia to occupied Crimea for a second day; Ukraine's General Staff
   said the road arch and a span of the road section collapsed. Reports
   dated 18 Nov 00:32 UTC and later. Sources: Reuters via BusinessWorld
   (18 Nov), Bloomberg via Japan Times (18 Nov), ISW campaign assessment
   for 17 Nov, NY Post.
3. **Xi opens APEC in Shenzhen.** Reuters, Shenzhen, 18 Nov 03:23 UTC:
   Xi Jinping urged support for "free and open trade" and opposed
   protectionism at the APEC CEO Summit; Putin had landed overnight and
   Trump was due later on Wednesday; the three leaders were set for
   talks on Thursday on the Iran and Ukraine wars, trade, AI and
   nuclear weapons. Also: Asia Daily on Xi's 18 Nov speech (tariff cuts,
   wider market access).
4. **Peltola wins Alaska.** The Associated Press called the race on
   Tuesday evening US time; Reuters reported it 18 Nov 03:27 UTC.
   Peltola defeated incumbent Dan Sullivan, giving Democrats a 56-44
   Senate majority; first Democrat to win a Senate race in Alaska since
   2008 and the first Alaska Native to serve in the US Senate.
   Sources: Reuters via SRN News / KWSN (18 Nov), NPR member stations,
   Focal News (18 Nov).
5. **Cyclone Xavier, Category 5.** Fiji Meteorological Service special
   weather bulletin No. 25, issued 18 Nov 02:56 UTC: Severe Tropical
   Cyclone Xavier, Category 5, near 10.1S 163.7W, sustained winds about
   215 km/h, gusts to 295 km/h; gale warnings for Pukapuka and Nassau.
   MetService bulletin issued 12:08pm Wed 18 Nov (NZDT) put maximum
   winds at 220 km/h; RNZ / One Turn reported gusts up to 315 km/h and
   shelters activated. The poster uses the 315 km/h gust figure as
   reported by RNZ via One Turn.
6. **Ebola in DR Congo.** UN News (17 Nov, reported in the 18 Nov daily
   editions): WHO Director-General Tedros said the outbreak had more
   than doubled in three months, with more than 11,000 cases and 5,400
   deaths in 67 health zones across eight provinces. Joint
   Africa CDC / UN statement (15-16 Nov) gave the same totals.
   Sources: UN News, ReliefWeb, OutbreakWatch (checked 18 Nov 02:58 UTC).

## "Also today" strip

- **An-12 crash, Siberia.** Russia's Defence Ministry said all 19 aboard
  a military An-12 transport died when it crashed near Kemerovo on
  Tuesday night; widely reported 18 Nov (Munich Eye 18 Nov 04:00 UTC,
  ANI via Daily Prabhat 18 Nov, AnewZ 18 Nov).
- **Gaza aid call.** Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK jointly called
  on Israel to grant immediate, unrestricted humanitarian access across
  Gaza, citing flooding. Reported 18 Nov 05:00 (The Paris Eye; also in
  the 18 Nov morning roundups citing Al Jazeera / WFP / IOM).
- **Nations League.** Norway beat Denmark 3-2 (Haaland penalty in
  stoppage time) and Portugal beat Wales 3-2; matches played Tuesday
  evening in Europe, reported Wednesday morning WITA, 18 Nov.
  Source: Aktualita (18 Nov).
